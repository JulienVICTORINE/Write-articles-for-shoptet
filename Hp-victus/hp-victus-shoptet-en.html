🎮 HP Gaming Laptops

HP Victus — The affordable gaming laptop from HP

Launched in 2021, the HP Victus is HP’s entry-level and mid-range gaming lineup. Positioned below the OMEN series, it targets gamers who want to enjoy the latest games without breaking their budget. Clean design, solid performance, and competitive pricing — Victus is the smart choice for getting into PC gaming without sacrificing the essentials.

🎯 Overview of the lineup

The HP Victus positions itself as the affordable gaming laptop from HP, sitting between the consumer Pavilion (without a dedicated GPU) and the high-end OMEN series. It targets student gamers, casual players, and users who want a single laptop for both work and gaming without compromise.

The Victus lineup is available in 15.6 and 16.1 inches, featuring NVIDIA GeForce RTX GPUs ranging from RTX 3050 to RTX 4060, as well as AMD Radeon options depending on the configuration. Displays typically offer a 144 Hz Full HD refresh rate, which is sufficient for smooth gaming in most titles. Unlike OMEN, Victus adopts a clean and understated design, without aggressive RGB or flashy gaming aesthetics.

Its main strength is its value for money: it delivers real gaming performance at a much more accessible price than direct competitors. It is also quieter and cooler than OMEN outside of intensive gaming sessions, making it a good all-around laptop for mixed work and gaming use.

🔍 Victus or OMEN — what’s the difference?

These two HP gaming lines are often compared. Here’s what you need to know:

🎯 HP Victus
Accessible gaming — RTX 3050 to 4060 GPUs, 144 Hz Full HD displays, clean and discreet design, lightweight plastic chassis. For gamers who want solid performance on a budget. Quieter and more versatile than OMEN for mixed work + gaming usage.
🔥 HP OMEN
High-end gaming — RTX 4060 to 4090 GPUs, up to 240 Hz QHD displays, Thermal Grizzly cooling, aluminum chassis, customizable RGB. For demanding gamers who want maximum performance. Heavier, more expensive, and noisier under load.
💡 Summary: choose the Victus if you play occasionally or have a limited budget. Choose OMEN if you play intensively, stream, or want to run the latest games at ultra settings.

👍 Advantages & drawbacks

The HP Victus is designed for accessible gaming and everyday mixed use — here are its strengths and honest limitations.

✔ Advantages
  • Excellent value for money — the best HP gaming laptop for tight budgets
  • Dedicated NVIDIA GeForce RTX GPU — real gaming performance in most popular titles
  • 144 Hz Full HD display — much smoother than a standard laptop for gaming
  • Clean and discreet design — suitable for school, office, or travel without drawing attention
  • Versatile: office work by day, gaming at night on the same machine
  • Quieter than the OMEN outside of intensive gaming sessions
  • Upgradeable RAM and SSD — easy upgrades after purchase
  • AMD FreeSync compatible — reduces tearing for smoother visuals
✘ Drawbacks
  • Fully plastic chassis — less premium finish than OMEN
  • Limited performance on the latest AAA games at high settings
  • Only 144 Hz Full HD display — no QHD or 240 Hz options
  • No customizable RGB — white keyboard backlight only
  • Limited battery life: 4–6 hours for office use, 2–3 hours gaming
  • Heavy for mobility: 2.2 to 2.5 kg + charger
  • Less efficient cooling than OMEN — runs hotter under sustained load
  • No OMEN Gaming Hub — less advanced performance management software
💡 Good to know: like all gaming laptops, performance is optimal when plugged in. On battery, the GPU automatically reduces performance to save power — which can impact the gaming experience.

📋 Main versions of the lineup

HP Victus 15 — The classic format

The entry-level model in the Victus lineup, with a 15.6-inch display. Available with NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 to RTX 4060 GPUs or AMD Radeon RX 6500M / 7600M depending on configurations. Powered by Intel Core i5/i7 or AMD Ryzen 5/7 processors. It is the most affordable and best-selling Victus — ideal for student gamers and casual players looking for their first gaming laptop.

HP Victus 16 — The larger format

The 16.1-inch version of the Victus, offering a slightly larger display and more space for cooling. It generally features more powerful GPU configurations than the 15-inch model (RTX 4060 or RTX 4070 on higher-end versions), better thermal performance, and a keyboard with a numeric keypad. Recommended for users who mostly play at home and prefer a larger screen.

HP Victus — AMD Ryzen versions

Some Victus models are equipped with AMD Ryzen 5 or Ryzen 7 processors paired with AMD Radeon RX GPUs. These configurations often provide better performance/value than Intel + NVIDIA versions for AMD-optimized games, and benefit from AMD FreeSync technology for smoother visuals. A good option if your budget is limited and you mainly play non-AAA titles.

✅ Our opinion

The HP Victus perfectly fulfills its role: delivering a real and accessible gaming experience in a versatile laptop that can also remain discreet in the office or classroom. For Fortnite, Valorant, FIFA, indie games, and less demanding titles, it is more than sufficient — at a much lower price than the OMEN.

If your budget allows and you play recent AAA games at high settings, consider upgrading to the HP OMEN 16 — the performance difference is worth it. But for a first gaming laptop or mixed work/gaming usage, the Victus 15 or 16 with RTX 4060 is currently one of the best choices on the market in its price range.
