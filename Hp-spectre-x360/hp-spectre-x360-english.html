💻 HP laptops

HP Spectre x360 — HP’s ultra-premium flagship

The HP Spectre x360 is the most high-end laptop in the entire HP consumer lineup. With its chamfered aluminum chassis, 2.8K or 4K OLED touchscreen display, 360° hinge, and up to 17 hours of battery life, it represents the very best HP has to offer. A direct competitor to the Dell XPS, MacBook Pro, and Lenovo Yoga Slim, it reaches a level of finish rarely seen in Windows devices.

✨ Overview of the lineup

The HP Spectre x360 sits at the top of the HP range, above the Envy and Pavilion series. It is designed for users who want the very best without compromise: refined design, top-quality display, and strong performance in the thinnest possible chassis.

Available in 13.5-inch and 14-inch versions, the Spectre x360 stands out with its signature beveled edges in aluminum, its 360° hinge allowing use in laptop, tablet, tent, or presentation mode, and its OLED touchscreen as standard on recent configurations. The rechargeable HP stylus is included or available as an option depending on the model.

The latest generations feature Intel Core Ultra processors with an integrated NPU for Windows AI features (Copilot+). Build quality exceeds anything else HP offers outside its professional EliteBook lineup.

👤 Who is the HP Spectre x360 for?

The Spectre x360 is a premium laptop designed for very specific user profiles. It is not meant for everyone:

💡 In short: the HP Spectre x360 is designed for users who see their laptop as a premium productivity tool and are willing to invest in the best possible everyday experience.

👍 Pros & cons

The HP Spectre x360 aims for excellence — here’s what it does extremely well and the few limitations to be aware of before buying.

✔ Pros
  • Exceptional design — chamfered aluminum chassis with flawless finish
  • 2.8K OLED touchscreen — top-tier colors, contrast, and brightness
  • Durable 360° hinge — 4 usage modes (laptop, tablet, tent, presentation)
  • HP stylus included or available — ideal for note-taking and drawing
  • Up to 17 hours of battery life — among the best on the market
  • Latest Intel Core Ultra processor with integrated AI NPU
  • High-quality Bang & Olufsen audio — the best sound in the HP lineup
  • FHD webcam with privacy shutter and noise reduction
  • Lightweight for a 14-inch device — around 1.4 kg only
✘ Cons
  • High price — the most expensive consumer HP laptop
  • No dedicated GPU — not suitable for gaming or heavy 3D workloads
  • Limited connectivity — 2× Thunderbolt 4 + 1 USB-A, no SD card reader
  • Limited to 14-inch maximum — no larger screen option available
  • RAM is soldered and not upgradable after purchase
  • Slight heat under sustained load despite thin chassis
  • OLED touchscreen can attract fingerprints easily
  • Overpriced if you don’t use the 360° hinge or stylus features
💡 Best choice if: you’re looking for an ultra-premium Windows laptop for work, travel, and versatile stylus use. If you don’t need the 360° hinge, the HP Envy 14 with OLED option offers a very similar experience at a significantly lower price.

📋 Main Spectre x360 versions

HP Spectre x360 13.5-inch

The most compact and lightweight model in the lineup — ideal for frequent travelers. Its 3:2 aspect ratio display (taller than the classic 16:9) is very comfortable for reading documents, browsing the web, or working on spreadsheets. It is slightly less powerful than the 14-inch version due to thermal constraints from its smaller chassis.

HP Spectre x360 14-inch

The most popular and recommended version. It combines a high-quality 2.8K OLED touchscreen, the latest Intel Core Ultra processors, up to 17 hours of battery life, and a weight of around 1.4 kg. It represents the best balance in the lineup between performance, battery life, and portability.

HP Spectre x360 16-inch (previous generation)

HP previously offered a 16-inch version of the Spectre x360 in earlier generations, with an optional NVIDIA discrete GPU. This model is no longer part of the 2024–2025 lineup, as HP has refocused the Spectre series on compact 13.5- and 14-inch formats. Users needing a large premium display are now directed toward the Envy 16.

✅ Our verdict

The HP Spectre x360 is simply one of the best Windows laptops available on the market. Its OLED display, build quality, and versatility make it a hard choice to beat for anyone looking for a premium Windows ultrabook.

However, it is important to be honest: its high price is fully justified only if you actually make use of the 360° hinge and touchscreen. If you use your laptop strictly in traditional mode, the HP Envy 14 with OLED will deliver about 90% of the experience for a much more reasonable budget.
