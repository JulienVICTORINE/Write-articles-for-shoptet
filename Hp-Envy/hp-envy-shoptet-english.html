💻 HP Laptops

HP Envy — Affordable premium for creatives and professionals

The HP Envy is HP’s high-end consumer lineup. Positioned between the Pavilion (mid-range) and the Spectre x360 (ultra-premium), the Envy targets users who want a sleek, well-built, and powerful laptop without paying a premium price. Aluminum design, high-quality display, Bang & Olufsen certified sound — the Envy is made for those who don’t want to compromise on everyday experience.

💎 Line overview

The HP Envy occupies the premium consumer segment in HP’s lineup. It immediately stands out from the Pavilion with its brushed aluminum chassis, which is more durable and elegant, as well as the quality of its displays — available in OLED or 2K options depending on the model. It’s the HP laptop you choose when you want something stylish on your desk.

The Envy lineup is available in 13, 14, and 16 inches, with IPS Full HD, OLED 2.8K, or 2K panels depending on configurations. Recent models feature Intel Core Ultra or AMD Ryzen 7/9 8000 series processors, with optional dedicated NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 or 4050 GPUs on 16-inch versions.

The Envy is also available in a convertible version, the Envy x360, featuring a 360° hinge and a touchscreen, allowing it to switch from laptop mode to tablet mode. This version is particularly popular among creatives and design students who use an HP stylus.

👍 Pros & cons

The HP Envy is designed for creative and everyday professional use — here are its strengths and limitations.

✔ Pros
  • Brushed aluminum chassis — premium, sturdy, and elegant finish
  • Optional OLED or 2K display — vibrant colors, deep blacks
  • Bang & Olufsen certified sound — audio quality above average
  • Solid performance for content creation and advanced productivity
  • x360 version with touchscreen and HP pen compatibility
  • FHD webcam with built-in physical privacy shutter
  • Good battery life: 8 to 12 hours depending on model and usage
  • Full connectivity: USB-C (Thunderbolt), USB-A, HDMI, SD card reader
  • Dedicated GPU available on 16-inch models for graphic workloads
✘ Cons
  • Higher price than the Pavilion — you pay for quality
  • Noticeable heat under sustained load (video rendering, multitasking)
  • Medium weight: 1.4 to 1.8 kg depending on model — not the lightest ultrabook
  • Limited dedicated GPU — not suitable for demanding gaming or professional 3D work
  • Soldered RAM on some models — no upgrade after purchase
  • No durability certification — less resistant to shocks or drops
  • Noticeable fan noise under load — less silent than the Spectre
  • No native RJ45 (Ethernet) port — requires an adapter
💡 Creative and professional use: for photo editing in Lightroom, creating polished presentations, light video editing, or simply working in a café with a stylish laptop, the HP Envy is an excellent choice. It is not designed for heavy gaming or very demanding professional workloads.

📋 Main versions of the lineup

HP Envy 14

The compact version of the lineup — 14 inches, lightweight (around 1.4 kg) and elegant. It features an IPS or 2.8K OLED display, Intel Core Ultra or AMD Ryzen 7 processors, and one of the best battery lives in the series. Ideal for mobile creatives who don’t want to carry a large laptop.

HP Envy 16

The large format of the lineup — 16 inches with a more comfortable 16:10 aspect ratio for productivity. It is the only model in the range offering an optional dedicated GPU (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 / 4050), making it a great choice for photo editing, video editing, or graphic design. Slightly heavier (around 1.9 kg), but the larger screen more than makes up for it.

HP Envy x360 (2-in-1)

The convertible version of the Envy, featuring a 360° hinge and an OLED or IPS touchscreen. Compatible with the rechargeable HP pen for note-taking or drawing directly on the screen. Available in 13.3 and 14-inch sizes. Slightly thicker than the clamshell version, but the added versatility is a real advantage for creatives and students.

HP Envy Move

An unusual model in the lineup: a portable all-in-one PC with a built-in battery and a 23.8-inch display. It can be moved from room to room without cables and used like a mobile desktop computer. A unique device in HP’s range, designed for users who want a large screen without the constraints of a fixed workstation.

✅ Our opinion

The HP Envy is the HP laptop that offers the best balance between build quality, performance, and price. It perfectly fills the gap between the overly plastic Pavilion and the overpriced Spectre — exactly where most demanding users are.

If you’re hesitating between the Envy and the Spectre, ask yourself: do you need the thinnest possible design and the highest-end display quality? If yes, the Spectre is worth the extra cost. Otherwise, the Envy 14 or 16 with OLED option will give you a very similar experience for much less money.
