💻 HP Laptops

HP Chromebook — Simple, fast and secure with ChromeOS

The HP Chromebook runs on ChromeOS, Google's operating system — not Windows. Designed for simplicity, speed, and security, it is widely used in schools, non-profits, and by users who mainly work in a web browser and Google apps. Boots in seconds, automatic updates, almost no viruses — Chromebook changes how you use a computer.

🎓 Product overview

The HP Chromebook is a unique category in HP’s lineup: it’s not a lightweight Windows laptop, but a device built around ChromeOS, a fast, lightweight system focused on the web and cloud. All Google apps are available natively — Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, Drive — and the Google Play Store allows Android apps on most recent models.

HP is one of the largest Chromebook manufacturers worldwide, especially thanks to education partnerships. Models range from affordable plastic devices for classrooms to HP Chromebook Plus models with aluminum chassis and high-quality Full HD displays for businesses and remote workers.

HP Chromebooks come in 11.6, 14, and 15.6 inches, including 2-in-1 versions (Chromebook x360). They use Intel Celeron, Intel Core i3/i5, or MediaTek processors depending on configuration — enough for web and cloud productivity, but not for heavy local workloads.

🔍 ChromeOS vs Windows — what's the difference?

Before buying a Chromebook, it's essential to understand how it differs from a traditional Windows laptop:

🎓 ChromeOS (Chromebook)
Lightweight system focused on web and cloud. Boots in 8 seconds, silent automatic updates, strong security, almost no viruses. Ideal for Google Workspace, web browsing, and Android apps. Cannot run Windows software (.exe) — no Word, Excel, Photoshop, or classic PC games.
🪟 Windows (Traditional laptop)
Full-featured system compatible with all professional software. Required for specialized tools, CAD, accounting, PC gaming, and enterprise apps. Heavier, can slow down over time, and requires antivirus. More expensive for equivalent hardware.
💡 The key question: do you need specific Windows software? If yes, choose a Windows laptop. If you mainly use web apps and Google Workspace, a Chromebook will be faster, simpler, and cheaper.

👍 Advantages & Drawbacks

The HP Chromebook is designed for simplicity, speed, and everyday security — here are its real strengths and important limitations.

✔ Advantages
  • Ultra-fast boot — under 10 seconds from power button to home screen
  • Very affordable — the cheapest HP laptop category available
  • Strong security — no viruses, silent automatic updates
  • Excellent battery life — up to 12–14 hours on recent models
  • Lightweight and compact — perfect for school bags and daily travel
  • Built-in Google Workspace — Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, Drive ready instantly
  • Access to Android apps via the Google Play Store
  • Almost no maintenance — no slowdown over time, no system cleanup needed
  • Easy centralized management via Google Admin Console — ideal for schools
✘ Drawbacks
  • Not compatible with Windows software (.exe) — no exceptions
  • Limited internal storage: 32 to 128 GB depending on the model
  • Heavy reliance on cloud and internet for most features
  • No traditional PC gaming — no Steam or AAA titles
  • Limited ChromeOS support lifespan — each model has an AUE (Auto Update Expiration) date
  • Android apps are sometimes poorly optimized for laptop screens
  • Entry-level processors — slowdowns with many tabs open
  • No full Microsoft Office — web versions only
💡 Important — AUE date: every Chromebook has an Auto Update Expiration date. After this, it no longer receives security updates. Always check the AUE before buying — some recent models are supported until 2030 or beyond.

📋 Main versions of the lineup

HP Chromebook 11 — The education format

The most compact and affordable HP Chromebook, with an 11.6-inch display. Designed for students, it’s durable, lightweight (under 1 kg), resistant to minor drops, and easy to manage via Google Admin Console. MediaTek or Intel Celeron processor, 4 GB RAM, 32 or 64 GB eMMC storage. Ideal for note-taking, Google Classroom, and video calls.

HP Chromebook 14 — The versatile option

The best-selling model in the range, with a 14-inch display. A good balance between screen size, portability, and price. Available with Intel Celeron or Intel Core i3 processors, 4 or 8 GB RAM, and Full HD IPS displays on newer versions. Ideal for students, families, and remote workers.

HP Chromebook x360 — The convertible version

The 2-in-1 Chromebook with a 360° hinge and touchscreen. Available in 11.6 and 14 inches, it can be used as a tablet, for drawing with a stylus, or for watching videos in tent mode. Very popular in education for its flexibility.

HP Chromebook Plus — The premium version

HP’s premium Chromebook lineup launched in 2023 with Google. Meets Chromebook Plus requirements: Full HD display minimum, 8 GB RAM, 128 GB SSD, Intel Core i3 or higher, FHD webcam, and Google AI features. Some models feature aluminum builds. Ideal for remote work and professionals.

✅ Our opinion

The HP Chromebook is an excellent device if it matches your usage. If you live in Google Workspace, browse the web, stream content, and make video calls, it delivers a faster, simpler, and more secure experience than an equivalent entry-level Windows laptop — often at a lower price.

However, if you need specific Windows software, large local storage, or professional applications, go for an HP Essential or HP Pavilion with Windows. The Chromebook is not a versatile laptop — it’s a specialized tool that excels in its domain.
