Hewlett-Packard — HP

Founded in a garage in Palo Alto in 1939, HP is one of the oldest technology companies in the world and one of the largest manufacturers of PCs and printers on the planet.

Overview

Who is HP? 🏢

Hewlett-Packard — better known as HP — is an American company founded in 1939 by Bill Hewlett and Dave Packard in the famous garage of Packard's home in Palo Alto, California. This garage is now considered the birthplace of Silicon Valley. Their first commercial product was an audio oscillator (the HP 200A), notably sold to Walt Disney Studios for the post-production of Fantasia.

For decades, HP established itself as a pioneer in measurement instruments, scientific calculators, laser printers, and personal computers. In 2002, HP merged with Compaq for $25 billion, becoming at the time the world's leading PC manufacturer and significantly strengthening its server and IT services offerings.

In 2015, the company carried out a historic split into two separate entities: HP Inc., which took over PCs and printers, and Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE), which handles servers, storage, networking, and enterprise services. This article focuses on HP Inc..

Today, HP Inc. is the second or third largest PC manufacturer in the world depending on the quarter, with a market share ranging between 20% and 22%, behind Lenovo and closely competing with Dell. HP Inc. is headquartered in Palo Alto, California, and operates in more than 170 countries.

Key History 📅

Major Milestones of HP

1939

Bill Hewlett and Dave Packard founded HP in a Palo Alto garage with $538 in capital. Their first product: the HP 200A audio oscillator, sold to Walt Disney.

1972

HP launched the HP-35, the world's first pocket scientific calculator, revolutionizing engineering and research.

1984

Introduction of the HP LaserJet, the first mass-market laser printer. It redefined office printing and remained a pillar of HP 's business for 40 years.

1994

Launch of the HP Pavilion, a consumer PC line that became one of the best-selling in the world in the 90s and 2000s.

2002

Merger with Compaq for $25 billion. HP gained iPAQ, Compaq Presario lines and a dominant position in the x86 server market.

2006

HP surpassed Dell to become the world's leading PC manufacturer, a position it held until 2013 when Lenovo overtook it.

2012

HP acquired Autonomy for $11 billion — a deal that resulted in an $8.8 billion write-down and a major accounting scandal.

2015

Split into two independent entities: HP Inc. (PCs and printers) and Hewlett Packard Enterprise (servers, storage, services).

2020

HP Inc. weathered the pandemic thanks to the surge in PC demand driven by remote work and online education.

2023–2024

HP refocused its strategy on AI PCs, hardware security, and sustainable printing solutions.

Product Range

What HP Makes 🏭

HP Inc. structures its offerings around two main pillars: personal systems (laptops, notebooks, desktop computers, workstations, monitors) and printing solutions (inkjet, laser, and large-format printers). The brand also offers accessories, hardware security, and managed services.

💻

Laptops

From entry-level Chromebooks to premium Spectre x360, including professional EliteBooks and OMEN gaming laptops — HP covers all segments with clearly positioned product lines.

🖥️

Desktop Computers

From ProDesk and EliteDesk models for businesses to consumer OmniDesk models, including the Z series workstations for creatives and engineers.

🖱️

Monitors

E-Series displays for office use, Z-Series for creatives and workstations, and OMEN for gaming — with sizes ranging from 21 to 40 inches.

🖨️

Printers & Printing

HP is the global leader in printing. Product lines include LaserJet (professional laser), DeskJet and ENVY (consumer inkjet), OfficeJet Pro (SMBs), DesignJet (large format), and HP Instant Ink (subscription service).

🔒

Security — HP Wolf Security

Hardware-based cybersecurity suite integrated into HP business PCs: firmware-level malware protection, threat isolation via micro-virtualization, and endpoint risk management.

🗄️

Servery — HPE (separate entity)

Since 2015, servers have been managed by Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE). The ProLiant, Synergy, and Apollo series cover the needs of data centers.

⌨️

Peripherals & Accessories

Keyboards, mice, webcams, Thunderbolt docks, headsets, and bags — HP offers a complete ecosystem of accessories aligned with its PCs.

🎮

Gaming — OMEN & Victus

Two gaming brands: OMEN for high-end gaming (RTX 4090 GPUs, 240 Hz displays, OMEN hub) and Victus for more affordable gaming with solid performance.

Summary

HP in a few words

Over 85 years of existence, HP has gone through every major technological revolution: from oscilloscopes to scientific calculators, from laser printers to premium laptops, from data center servers to AI PCs. Born in a Palo Alto garage, the company has become a symbol of American tech — and the birthplace of what would later be called Silicon Valley.

HP Inc.’s strength lies in the clarity and depth of its segmentation: Spectre for premium, Envy for stylish mid-range, EliteBook for secure business, OMEN for high-end gaming, Victus for accessible gaming, OmniBook for AI — each lineup has a clear identity and a well-defined target audience.

With its shift toward Copilot+ PCs, hardware-based security through HP Wolf, and AI integration across workflows, HP is positioning itself to remain a key player in a rapidly evolving market.
