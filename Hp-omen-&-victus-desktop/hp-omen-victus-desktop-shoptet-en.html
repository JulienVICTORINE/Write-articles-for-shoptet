🎮 HP Gaming Desktop PC

HP OMEN Desktop & Victus Desktop — HP Gaming Towers

HP offers two ranges of gaming desktop PCs: the OMEN Desktop for demanding gamers who want maximum power in a refined chassis, and the Victus Desktop for players looking for solid gaming performance at a controlled budget. Two different approaches, two distinct user profiles — here’s everything you need to know to choose.

🎯 Overview of the two ranges

Unlike gaming laptops which involve thermal compromises, gaming desktop towers offer complete freedom in terms of components and cooling. No size or battery constraints — the GPU can run at full power constantly, without thermal throttling. This is where gaming desktops truly shine for players who mostly stay at home.

The OMEN Desktop features the most powerful NVIDIA GeForce RTX GPUs — RTX 4070, 4080 or 4090 — in a chassis with advanced thermal management, customizable RGB lighting via OMEN Gaming Hub, and a design that allows easy upgrades (adding RAM, replacing the GPU, additional storage).

The Victus Desktop follows the philosophy of the Victus laptop: solid gaming performance with RTX 3060 to RTX 4060 GPUs at a much more affordable price.

🔥 OMEN Desktop
High-end gaming — RTX 4070 to 4090 GPUs, aluminum chassis with customizable RGB, optimized cooling for long sessions, OMEN Gaming Hub, designed for upgrades. For gamers who want maximum performance without compromise and play in 4K or high-refresh-rate 1440p.
🎯 Victus Desktop
Accessible gaming — RTX 3060 to RTX 4060 GPUs, clean chassis without excessive RGB, competitive pricing. For gamers who want to play comfortably in Full HD or 1440p without spending a fortune. Great value for money and easy to upgrade over time.
💡 Desktop vs gaming laptop advantage: at the same budget, a gaming desktop is always more powerful than a gaming laptop. The GPU can run at 100% capacity without thermal limits, cooling is far more efficient, and components can be replaced individually.

🔥 HP OMEN Desktop — Advantages & disadvantages

OMEN Desktop — High-end gaming

The OMEN Desktop is designed for demanding gamers who want the best possible experience at home.

✔ Advantages
  • NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 to 4090 GPUs — power for 4K ultra or 1440p 165+ Hz gaming
  • No thermal throttling — GPU runs at 100% performance continuously
  • Well-ventilated chassis with glass panel — bold gaming design and efficient cooling
  • Customizable RGB via OMEN Gaming Hub — lighting tailored to your preferences
  • Easily upgradeable — GPU, RAM, and storage can be replaced individually
  • Full gaming connectivity: USB-A ×4, USB-C, HDMI 2.1, DisplayPort, RJ45
  • OMEN Gaming Hub — real-time monitoring, performance and network optimization
  • Quiet at idle — active cooling only under gaming load
  • Multi-monitor support — up to 3 displays depending on GPU
✘ Disadvantages
  • High price — RTX 4090 configurations easily exceed €2,500
  • Bulky — requires space on or under your desk
  • High power consumption — 500W to 850W depending on configuration
  • Loud under heavy gaming load — noticeable fan noise
  • Very pronounced gaming design — not discreet in a living room or office
  • Requires separate monitor, keyboard, and mouse — additional cost
  • No MIL-SPEC certification or enterprise security features
  • Overkill if you mainly play lightweight games
💡 OMEN Desktop summary: for playing Cyberpunk 2077, Alan Wake 2 or Black Myth Wukong in 4K ultra, streaming in HD while gaming, or simply having the best possible desktop gaming setup — the OMEN Desktop is a long-term investment worth making.

🎯 HP Victus Desktop — Advantages & disadvantages

Victus Desktop — Affordable gaming

The Victus Desktop is designed for gamers who want essential gaming performance at a controlled price, with room for future upgrades.

✔ Advantages
  • Excellent value for money — best HP gaming desktop for tight budgets
  • NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 to 4060 GPUs — smooth gaming in Full HD and 1440p
  • Better performance than a gaming laptop at the same price
  • Clean and discreet design — fits easily in a living room
  • Upgradeable: GPU, RAM, and SSD easily replaceable
  • Moderate power consumption — more efficient than OMEN
  • AMD FreeSync compatible — smooth visuals without tearing
  • Quiet outside of intensive gaming sessions
✘ Disadvantages
  • Limited GPU for latest AAA games in ultra or 4K
  • No RGB lighting — simple design but no customization
  • Plastic chassis — less premium than OMEN
  • No OMEN Gaming Hub — less advanced performance software
  • Fewer front I/O ports compared to OMEN
  • Still bulky — requires desk space
  • Requires separate monitor, keyboard, and mouse
  • Not ideal for high-quality streaming while gaming
💡 Victus Desktop summary: for playing Fortnite, Valorant, FIFA, GTA V or indie games in Full HD 144 Hz on a reasonable budget, the Victus Desktop is an excellent entry point into desktop gaming. Its RTX 4060 will comfortably last 2–3 years before requiring an upgrade.

📋 Main versions

HP OMEN 45L — The high-end gaming tower

The flagship model of the OMEN Desktop range. Its large, spacious chassis features a patented thermal management system that physically separates the GPU chamber from the CPU chamber, ensuring optimal cooling even during long gaming sessions. Compatible with RTX 4080 and 4090 GPUs, it supports up to 64GB of DDR5 RAM and multiple NVMe SSDs. Its tempered glass side panel and OMEN RGB lighting make it one of the most visually impressive gaming towers in HP’s lineup.

HP OMEN 35L — The compact gaming tower

A more compact version of the OMEN Desktop, slightly less bulky than the 45L. It offers the same gaming features — RTX 4070 or 4080 GPUs, customizable RGB, OMEN Gaming Hub — in a more discreet format. Recommended for gamers who want OMEN power without the XXL size of the 45L. Some compromises on the number of additional storage slots.

HP Victus Desktop TG01 — The affordable gaming tower

HP’s entry-level gaming desktop. Compact format, clean chassis with subtle RGB accents, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 to RTX 4060 GPUs depending on the configuration, Intel Core i5 or i7 processor. Its main strength: excellent value for money for a first gaming desktop, with easy upgrade options for GPU or RAM. Ideal for gamers transitioning from a gaming laptop to a desktop without breaking the bank.

✅ Our opinion

Whether you choose the OMEN or the Victus Desktop, you are opting for a more powerful and upgradeable gaming experience than a laptop at the same budget. A gaming desktop is always the best option for sedentary gamers — with equivalent specs, it will be more powerful, quieter under load, and last longer.

Choose the OMEN 35L or 45L if you want to play in 4K or 1440p with the latest GPUs and plan to keep your PC for several years. Choose the Victus Desktop TG01 if you are new to desktop gaming or have a limited budget — you can upgrade the GPU in 2 to 3 years to keep up with new games without replacing the entire system.
