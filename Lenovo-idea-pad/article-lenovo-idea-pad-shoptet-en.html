The IdeaPad range from Lenovo is one of the most popular in the consumer market. Affordable, versatile, and available in many configurations, it caters to a wide range of users.

Overview of the range

The IdeaPad is Lenovo's consumer-oriented range, positioned between entry-level Chromebooks and professional ThinkPad laptops. It covers a wide spectrum of needs: light office work, web browsing, multimedia, and even gaming with higher-end series.

The range is available in several sub-series (IdeaPad 1, 3, 5, Slim 5, Flex…), with screen sizes ranging from 14 to 16 inches and Intel, AMD, or Qualcomm processors depending on the model. There is something for every budget, generally between €350 and €900.

Who is the IdeaPad for?

The IdeaPad is primarily aimed at students and everyday users looking for a reliable computer without breaking the bank. It is also a good choice for families needing a second PC or for people who mainly use their computer for browsing, watching videos, or working on documents.

Mid-range versions (IdeaPad Slim 5, IdeaPad 5) are also suitable for occasional remote workers who do not need the advanced features of a professional computer.

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

  • Excellent value for money
  • Wide choice of models and configurations
  • Good battery life on recent models
  • Clean and relatively lightweight design
  • Widely available in stores and online

Drawbacks

  • Plastic finishes on entry-level models
  • Limited performance for intensive use
  • Sometimes disappointing display on low-end models
  • Preinstalled software (bloatware) to remove

Our opinion

The IdeaPad is a safe choice for anyone looking for a laptop without a high budget constraint. Lenovo has built a consistent range, regularly updated, that delivers on everyday use. For students, occasional remote workers, or family use, it is a choice that is hard to go wrong with. We particularly recommend the IdeaPad Slim 5 models, which offer the best balance between price, performance, and build quality.
