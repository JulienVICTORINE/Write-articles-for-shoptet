💻 Lenovo Laptops

Lenovo ThinkBook — The accessible professional, without compromising on essentials

The ThinkBook is the professional laptop range from Lenovo designed for SMEs, freelancers, and professionals who want the reliability and seriousness of a ThinkPad without paying a premium price. Launched in 2019, it quickly established itself as a rational and popular choice for those who refuse to sacrifice quality for affordability.

🏆 Product Overview

The ThinkBook holds a strategic position in Lenovo's catalog: above the consumer IdeaPad range, and below the premium business ThinkPad line. It inherits the professional DNA of the Think family — durability, high-quality keyboard, and generous connectivity — while offering a more modern design and significantly more affordable pricing.

Recognizable by its light gray brushed aluminum chassis, the ThinkBook stands out from the austere matte black ThinkPads. It features a clean yet refined look, suitable for both corporate offices and coworking spaces. The range mainly includes 14 and 16-inch displays, with configurations based on Intel Core Ultra, AMD Ryzen, or Qualcomm Snapdragon depending on the model, and a strong focus on battery life, connectivity, and scalability (replaceable RAM and SSD on most models).

📜 ThinkBook positioning: launched in 2019 to fill the gap between consumer IdeaPad and premium professional ThinkPad, the ThinkBook addresses a clear market demand. SMEs and freelancers wanted Think quality without the extra costs associated with military certifications and enterprise fleet management features. Lenovo designed the ThinkBook precisely for them.

👍 Pros & Cons

The ThinkBook is optimized for everyday office productivity — here are its well-known strengths and the limitations to consider before buying.

✔ Pros
  • Excellent keyboard with integrated numeric keypad — typing comfort clearly above typical consumer laptops
  • Solid aluminum chassis — durable build quality designed to last
  • Outstanding battery life — up to 20 hours on 16" models depending on configuration
  • Large 16" display with 16:10 aspect ratio — more vertical workspace, ideal for productivity
  • Strong value for money compared to Dell Vostro, HP ProBook, or ASUS ExpertBook
  • Full connectivity: USB-A, USB-C, HDMI, SD card reader, audio jack — nothing missing
  • Replaceable RAM and SSD on most models — long-term upgradability
  • Quiet fan and efficient thermal management — comfortable during extended use
✘ Cons
  • Display with limited color accuracy — not recommended for graphic design, photo editing, or video work
  • Screen brightness could be better — under 300 nits, difficult to use in bright outdoor conditions
  • Slightly heavy for a 16" laptop — around 1.85 kg, not ideal for intensive daily mobility
  • Lid difficult to open with one hand — inconvenient when on the move
  • No MIL-SPEC certification — decent durability but below ThinkPad standards
  • No built-in 4G/5G option — relies on Wi-Fi or tethering
  • Pre-installed software (bloatware) can be intrusive — cleanup recommended on first use
  • Simple design without originality — not ideal for users wanting a bold-looking laptop
💡 Intensive office use: for writing reports, managing spreadsheets, attending video calls, or occasional remote work — the ThinkBook performs perfectly. Its reliability and keyboard make it a serious work tool at a justified price.

📋 Main series in the lineup

ThinkBook 14 — The versatile compact

The 14-inch format is the entry point into the ThinkBook range. Lightweight (around 1.4 kg) and easy to carry, it offers an excellent balance between portability and performance for everyday office use. Available with Intel Core Ultra or AMD Ryzen configurations, with up to 32 GB of RAM and fast NVMe SSD. Ideal for professionals who travel frequently and don’t want to compromise on keyboard quality and build.

ThinkBook 16 — Maximum productivity

The 16-inch model is the best-selling ThinkBook. Its large 16:10 display provides generous workspace, especially appreciated for spreadsheets, multitasking, and working with multiple windows simultaneously. It includes a full numeric keypad, extensive connectivity, and record-breaking battery life that can exceed 20 hours. The natural choice for those who mainly work at a desk or travel occasionally.

ThinkBook 14 2-in-1 — Flexibility first

The convertible version of the ThinkBook lineup, featuring a 360° hinge that allows the laptop to be used in tablet, tent, or presentation mode. Touchscreen display, optional stylus support, and a hybrid design tailored for creative professionals, trainers, and salespeople who need flexibility for presentations and note-taking. Slightly heavier than the standard version, but much more versatile in daily use.

ThinkBook Plus — Innovation for advanced users

The Plus series explores innovative concepts within the ThinkBook lineup. Some models feature a secondary E-Ink display on the lid, usable for note-taking, calendar display, or reading without turning on the main screen. Other versions offer dual-screen setups with an additional display above the keyboard. These models are designed for advanced users who want to push their productivity further with unique tools.

✅ Our opinion

The ThinkBook perfectly delivers on its promise: offering the essentials of the Lenovo professional experience at an accessible price. Its keyboard, durability, battery life, and connectivity make it a serious work tool that can be confidently recommended for everyday office use.

If you're hesitating between ThinkBook and ThinkPad: choose the ThinkBook if you're a freelancer, SME, or advanced student looking for the best value for money. Go for the ThinkPad if you have high security requirements, need MIL-SPEC certifications, or manage an enterprise IT fleet. In any case, you'll get a well-built laptop that will serve you efficiently for several years.
