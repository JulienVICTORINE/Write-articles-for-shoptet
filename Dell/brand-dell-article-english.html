Introduction

Who is Dell?

Dell was founded in 1984 in Austin, Texas by Michael Dell, then a student at the University of Texas. At the age of 19, he started the company from his dorm room with a simple yet revolutionary concept for its time: selling computers directly to customers without intermediaries. This direct sales model allowed him to offer cheaper and customizable computers.

In 2016, Dell completed one of the largest acquisitions in the history of information technology by purchasing EMC Corporation for $67 billion, leading to the creation of Dell Technologies. The company thus became a giant covering both consumer hardware and the most complex professional solutions.

Today, Dell Technologies operates in more than 180 countries, employs tens of thousands of people worldwide, and is regularly ranked among the largest companies in the Fortune 500.

Focus — Monitors

Dell Monitor Range

Dell is one of the most recognized monitor manufacturers on the market, offering a range that covers office, professional, and gaming use cases.

  • UltraSharp

    Dell’s premium lineup designed for graphics and visual professionals. UltraSharp monitors stand out for their excellent color accuracy (sRGB, DCI-P3, and Adobe RGB coverage), high resolutions (4K, 5K, or up to 8K), and factory calibration, making them ideal for photo editing and content creation. Available in sizes from 24 to 40 inches, they are also appreciated for their ergonomics: height, tilt, swivel, and pivot adjustments, as well as VESA mount compatibility.

    Included here

  • Gaming — Alienware & G-Series

    Dell targets the gaming market through two brands: Alienware for the high-end segment (QD-OLED, 360 Hz, 0.1 ms response time) and G-Series offering excellent value for money. These monitors support G-Sync or FreeSync Premium, with refresh rates ranging from 144 Hz to 360 Hz, IPS or OLED panels, and resolutions from Full HD to QHD and up to 4K depending on the model.

    Included here

  • P Series — Professional

    A versatile professional lineup widely used in businesses. P Series monitors offer full ergonomics (height, tilt, swivel, and pivot adjustments), integrated USB hubs, and connectivity suited to modern workstations (DisplayPort, HDMI, USB-C). IPS panels ensure good viewing angles and solid color performance for intensive office work. Available in sizes from 22 to 27 inches.

    Included here

  • S Series — Stylish

    The S Series is aimed at everyday users and households looking for a stylish monitor at an affordable price. The design is slim and clean, with thin bezels and a modern stand. Panels range from Full HD to 4K, using IPS or VA technology depending on the model. Compared to the P Series, it offers fewer adjustment options but makes up for it with an attractive design and competitive pricing.

Summary

Dell in a Few Words

From its humble beginnings in a student dorm room in Austin to its current status as a global IT giant, Dell has continuously adapted and innovated. As a pioneer of the direct sales model, it has established itself in both the consumer market and the most demanding enterprise environments.

Its strength lies in the diversity and consistency of its offering: each product line addresses a specific need — from Alienware gaming laptops to Precision mobile workstations, as well as UltraSharp monitors and PowerEdge servers.
