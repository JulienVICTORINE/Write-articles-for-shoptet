💻 HP Laptops

HP Essential — The affordable laptop to get started

Formerly known as HP Stream and HP 15, the HP Essential range is HP’s entry-level offering. It is designed for people who need a simple, low-cost, ready-to-use laptop, without specific requirements in terms of performance or design.

🏠 Range overview

The HP Essential sits at the base of HP’s lineup. It is positioned below the Pavilion (mid-range) and represents the most affordable entry point into the brand’s Windows laptops. It is the default choice for those primarily looking for a low price and hassle-free use.

The range is mainly available in 15.6 inches, with some 14-inch models. Displays are TN or IPS panels depending on the version, with Full HD resolution (1920×1080). The chassis is entirely plastic, simple, without fancy colors — usually dark gray or black.

These machines are equipped with entry-level processors (Intel Celeron, Pentium Silver or AMD Athlon / Ryzen 3) and limited storage (64 GB eMMC on older HP Stream models, 256 GB SSD on recent HP 15 models). They are designed for light, home use, mostly plugged in at home.

👍 Pros & Cons

The HP Essential is designed for light home use — here are its strengths and limitations in this context.

✔ Pros
  • Very low price — the cheapest HP laptop on the market
  • Ready to use out of the box with Windows 11
  • Lightweight for its size (around 1.7 kg)
  • Comfortable 15.6-inch Full HD display for watching videos
  • Decent battery life for home use (6 to 8 hours)
  • Quiet — discreet fan during light office tasks
  • Enough connectivity: USB-A, USB-C, HDMI, audio jack
  • Ideal as a secondary computer or backup machine
✘ Cons
  • Entry-level processor — slow as soon as multiple tasks pile up
  • Only 8 GB of RAM — quickly limiting with a browser open
  • Limited storage on older models (64 GB eMMC)
  • All-plastic chassis — basic finish, not very shock-resistant
  • TN panel on some models — poor viewing angles
  • No dedicated graphics card — not suitable for gaming
  • 720p webcam only — insufficient quality for professional video calls
  • Risk of slowdowns over time without upgrade options
💡 Home use: for browsing the internet, sending emails, watching series, or making occasional video calls, the HP Essential is sufficient. However, it quickly shows its limits when more performance is required.

📋 Main versions of the range

HP Stream (older generation)

The predecessor of the Essential range, launched in 2014 to compete with Chromebooks. Very affordable but limited by its 32 or 64 GB eMMC storage (slow flash memory) and 4 GB of RAM. It came with a Microsoft 365 subscription to compensate for the limited local storage. Now replaced by more recent HP 15 models.

HP 15 / HP Essential 15

The current version of the range. It offers a real 256 GB SSD (sometimes 512 GB), a recent AMD Ryzen 3 or Intel Core i3 processor, and 8 GB of RAM. Much more usable than the old Stream, it remains HP’s most affordable solution for basic Windows use at home.

HP Essential 14

The 14-inch format, more compact and slightly lighter. It uses the same components as the HP 15, but in a smaller chassis — ideal for people looking for a space-saving computer to place on a small table or compact desk.

✅ Our opinion

The HP Essential fulfills its purpose: offering a functional laptop at the lowest possible price. It is perfectly suited as a first computer for a child, a backup machine, or for very occasional home use.

However, if your budget allows it, we recommend going directly for the HP Pavilion: for a often modest price difference, you get a significantly faster processor, more RAM, and a much better overall experience that will last longer.
