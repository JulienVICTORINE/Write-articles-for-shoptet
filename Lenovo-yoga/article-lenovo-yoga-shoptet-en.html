Overview

The Lenovo Yoga range includes convertible laptops whose screen rotates 360°, allowing you to switch from classic PC mode to tablet mode in just a few seconds. It includes several families: the Yoga 7 and Yoga 9 for general users and creatives, the Yoga Slim for a more traditional ultraportable, and the Yoga Book for an ultra-compact format. All of them share attention to build quality, portability, and battery life.

Advantages and Drawbacks

Advantages

  • Sleek design and good build quality
  • Versatility thanks to 360° tablet mode
  • Good battery life
  • Bright and well-calibrated displays
  • Stylus support (depending on model)

Drawbacks

  • Often expensive
  • Limited graphics performance
  • Not suitable for gaming

Our opinion

The Lenovo Yoga is an excellent choice for versatile everyday use: well built, lightweight, and pleasant to use. The 360° hinge is a real advantage for switching modes without needing an additional tablet. However, if you are looking for raw power or gaming performance, you should look elsewhere.
