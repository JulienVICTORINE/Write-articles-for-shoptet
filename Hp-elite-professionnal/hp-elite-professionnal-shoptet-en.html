HP Elite Dragonfly & Elite x360 — HP’s ultra-light business ultrabooks

The HP Elite Dragonfly and HP Elite x360 are the two thinnest and lightest professional ultrabooks in HP’s lineup. Designed for executives, consultants, and sales professionals who spend their days on the move, they combine extreme portability, enterprise-grade security, and optimal connectivity — all in a premium chassis that rivals the best laptops on the market.

💼 Lineup overview

The HP Elite Dragonfly and HP Elite x360 belong to the EliteBook family, but stand out with an even more premium positioning. While the EliteBook 840 is the company’s versatile benchmark, the Dragonfly and Elite x360 are designed for users who prioritize lightness and mobility above all.

The Dragonfly is the lightest laptop in HP’s professional lineup — some models weigh under 990 grams. Its chassis is made from magnesium alloy and carbon fiber, materials used in aerospace for their exceptional rigidity and lightness. Despite its featherweight design, it still includes full MIL-SPEC certifications and the HP Wolf Security suite.

The Elite x360 is the convertible 2-in-1 version: its 360° hinge allows switching between laptop, tablet, tent, or presentation modes. It features a touchscreen and supports the HP stylus for digital signatures and note-taking. Both models optionally include integrated 4G or 5G connectivity for working anywhere without relying on Wi-Fi.

👍 Pros & Cons

These two ultrabooks share the same strengths and limitations — here is a common overview for both models.

✔ Pros
  • Extremely lightweight — Dragonfly under 990 g, x360 around 1.2 kg
  • Magnesium / carbon fiber chassis — strong, light, and premium
  • MIL-SPEC 810H certification — resistant to shocks, drops, and extreme conditions
  • Hardware-integrated HP Wolf Security — advanced protection against cyber threats
  • Sure View option — built-in privacy screen for public environments
  • Integrated 4G / 5G option — stay connected anywhere without Wi-Fi
  • Up to 16 hours of battery life — a full workday without a charger
  • FHD webcam with physical shutter and AI noise reduction
  • Subtle premium design — professional look that inspires confidence
✘ Cons
  • Very high price — among the most expensive HP business laptops
  • Limited ports — adapters (often Thunderbolt) are frequently required
  • No dedicated GPU — only integrated Intel Iris Xe or Arc graphics
  • Screen limited to 14 inches max — no large display option
  • Soldered RAM on all models — no upgrade possible after purchase
  • Expensive repairs — specific and rare components
  • Slight heat under sustained load due to thin chassis
  • Overkill if you mostly work at a desk
💡 Heavy mobile usage: for professionals constantly moving between meetings, trains, and airports, these laptops truly shine. Their light weight and battery life make a real difference — once you're used to it, going back to a 1.8 kg laptop feels difficult.

📋 Main versions

HP Elite Dragonfly G4 / G5 — 13.5 inches

The most compact and lightweight version — under 990 g on some configurations. Its 3:2 aspect ratio display is especially appreciated for reading documents and working with spreadsheets. Intel Core Ultra processor, up to 16 hours of battery life, and optional integrated 5G. The preferred choice for executives who travel by plane several times a week.

HP Elite Dragonfly G4 / G5 — 14 inches

The 14-inch format offers a slightly larger display while remaining very light (around 1.1 kg). It generally provides slightly more connectivity than the 13.5-inch model and better thermal management. Ideal for those who want the comfort of a 14-inch screen without sacrificing portability.

HP Elite x360 13.3 inches

A compact convertible format with a 360° hinge and a touchscreen (OLED or IPS). Compatible with the rechargeable HP stylus for note-taking, annotations, and electronic signatures. Highly appreciated by consultants and trainers who frequently present in tent or tablet mode. Slightly heavier than the Dragonfly due to the hinge.

HP Elite x360 14 inches

The 14-inch version of the convertible, offering the best balance between screen size and portability in the Elite x360 lineup. Available with an optional 2.8K OLED display for those who need superior image quality during client presentations. The recommended configuration for most mobile professionals looking for a convertible device.

✅ Our opinion

The HP Elite Dragonfly and Elite x360 are simply the best professional laptops HP produces in terms of lightness and mobility. Their build quality, integrated security, and battery life make them exceptional work companions for highly mobile professionals.

The choice between the two comes down to a simple question: do you need a touchscreen and a 360° hinge? If so, the Elite x360 14-inch is the best option. Otherwise, the Dragonfly 14-inch will offer slightly less weight and slightly better battery life for a very similar everyday experience.
